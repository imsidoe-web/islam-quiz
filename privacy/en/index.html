Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who are we?

NurSpark is an Islamic quiz app, made under the name Zeno Innovations. The person responsible for the processing of personal data is I. Idoe.

Questions about your data? Email info@nurspark.app.

In short

  • We ask for as little data as possible.
  • No ads and we sell nothing. Only if you agree do we count visits with Google Analytics.
  • We do not ask for your location.
  • Preferably choose a nickname: it is visible to other players.

Which data and why?

On your own device

Your player name, avatar, progress (levels, stars, badges), settings and the city you choose for the prayer times are stored in the browser of your device. The prayer times are calculated on your device itself; your city is not sent to us.

As a guest on the scoreboard

If you play as a guest, the app creates an anonymous guest account. On the worldwide scoreboard and the map, other players see your player name, avatar, score, badges and streak. No email address is needed.

With an account

If you create an account with email and password, we store your email address and a secured (encrypted) version of your password. If you log in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address and, if you have one, your profile photo from them. This way you can also use your progress on another device. Other players only see your first name or nickname, your profile photo (if you have one), your avatar and your score.

Group, Duel and Ajalat al-‘Ilm

If you play in a group, a duel or the Wheel of Knowledge, the group code, your player name, avatar and scores are shared with the players in that group. A request for a life as sadaqa also goes through the group.

Reminders

If you turn on notifications, we store a technical key of your browser with which we can send you a reminder for your daily streak. You can always turn this off in the settings of the app or your browser.

Technical data

As with any website, the server temporarily sees your IP address and browser type, in order to show and secure the app.

On what basis?

We use your data to make the app work the way you ask: saving your progress, the scoreboard, groups and duels (performance of the service). We only send notifications if you give your own consent.

Who do we share data with?

We do not sell data. For the technology we use these services, which process the data only for us:

  • Vercel: hosting of the app.
  • Supabase: accounts, logging in, scoreboard, streaks and reminders.
  • Google Firebase (servers in Europe): groups, duels and Ajalat al-‘Ilm.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: a captcha that checks that you are not a robot, when you start as a guest or log in with email. Cloudflare then sees your IP address and a few details about your browser.
  • Google and Meta (Facebook): only if you log in with their account.
  • jsDelivr: a software library. jsDelivr then sees your IP address. The fonts are on our own server, so nothing is sent to Google for them.

Some of these services may process data outside the European Union. For this they use the safeguards required by law, such as the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission.

Children

NurSpark is also made for children. That is why we are extra careful with their data.

How old are you?

At the first start the app asks how old you are: under 13, 13 to 16 or 16 or older. We only store that group on your own device, not your exact age or date of birth. You can change the group later via Settings → Age.

For children under 13

  • No profile photo: on the leaderboard and the map others only see a nickname and an avatar.
  • No logging in with Facebook.
  • No playing with strangers: playing online is only possible with family or friends, via a group, a code or a link. Searching for someone who is online now is not possible.
  • No chat: in NurSpark you cannot send messages to other players (this applies to everyone).
  • Parent check: before the app goes to another app or website (for example to share something via WhatsApp), the app asks a parent or carer to solve a short sum.
  • No visitor statistics: Google Analytics is off and the app does not ask for it.
  • No ads and no purchases (this applies to everyone).

Creating an account

If you are younger than 16, you need permission from a parent or carer before you create an account or log in with Google. Without an account you can simply play as a guest; no email address is needed for that.

For parents

Would you like to know what data about your child is stored, or have it deleted? Email info@nurspark.app. We reply within a month. You can also delete the account yourself in the app (see below). Data on the device itself you can erase via Settings → Erase progress or by clearing the browser data.

How long do we keep data?

We do not keep data longer than necessary. Your account, scores and badges are kept as long as you use the app or until you ask for them to be deleted. Data on your own device disappears when you erase the progress in the app or clear the browser data.

Your rights

You have the right to view your data, have it corrected or deleted, and to object. Email info@nurspark.app for this. We reply within a month. If you disagree with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself at any time in the app: Account & Log in → Delete account. Your account, your place on the leaderboard, your daily streak and your reminders are then erased immediately. If that does not work, email info@nurspark.app. You can find more explanation at nurspark.app/account-verwijderen.

Cookies and visitor statistics

NurSpark does not use advertising cookies. The app does store data in the storage of your browser (such as your progress and whether you are logged in), because the app would not work otherwise.

To see how many people use the app and which parts are popular, we use Google Analytics. This is only on if you give consent in the notice at your first visit. Google Analytics then places cookies (starting with _ga) and sees, among other things, which pages you view, your device and browser, and roughly which country or region you are in. Google does not store IP addresses in the EU. We have turned off sharing with other Google services and advertising features. The data is kept for a maximum of 2 months.

In addition, without cookies and without personal data, we count how often a part of the app is opened. That is just a number per part per day. It is not linked to you, your account or your device.

You can always change your choice in the app: Settings → Visitor statistics. If you turn it off, the Google Analytics cookies are erased.

Changes

If we change this privacy policy, you will see it from the date at the top of this page.
